## 3. Audience Insights Summary

### What This Audience Values

1. **Transparency** — They can see how the system works
2. **Efficiency** — Time saved is the #1 selling point
3. **Quality** — They'd rather have less content that's good than more that's mediocre
4. **Technical credibility** — The system must actually work, not just sound impressive
5. **Australian context** — Local references, Australian English, local market understanding
6. **Community** — They engage with peers, not just brands
7. **Data** — Numbers, sources, and evidence win arguments

### Content Gaps This Audience Has

1. **No single source** for cross-platform content strategy guidance
2. **No unified framework** that connects research → production → distribution → optimisation
3. **No AI disclosure standards** that Australian creators can confidently adopt
4. **No Australian-specific** platform compliance guides (Reddit, X, TikTok, etc.)
5. **No reproducible** process for turning one piece of research into a full campaign

### How Content Should Reach This Audience

- **LinkedIn**: Long-form articles about agent workflows, content strategy case studies
- **X/Twitter**: Threads breaking down the dependency model, quick tips, engagement bait
- **Reddit**: Genuine community participation, sharing insights, AMAs on agent systems
- **YouTube**: Walkthroughs of the system in action, prediction breakdowns, workflow tutorials
- **Email**: Weekly digest of new research, platform updates, product news
- **Obsidian**: Personal knowledge management, research vaults, process documentation

## 4. Audience Evolution

The audience is expected to grow through:

1. **Content marketing** — Publishing valuable content that attracts new audience members
2. **Community building** — Reddit, Discord, and LinkedIn groups
3. **Product adoption** — Content creators and publishers becoming users
4. **Word of mouth** — Satisfied users refer others
5. **Open source contribution** — Developers extending the agent framework

### Audience Growth Metrics to Track

- Content engagement rate per platform
- Follower/subscriber growth rate
- Content production velocity (articles per week)
- Agent workflow adoption rate
- Community participation (comments, shares, Discord messages)
- Conversion from free content to paid products/services
